# Data provenance

Runtime catalog: https://atlas.vanatome.vixotic.in/releases/1.4.0/catalog.json

The viewer loads the Vanatome full-body profile and defaults to skeletal, muscular, and cardiovascular layers.

The atlas is derived from Z-Anatomy / BodyParts3D. Geometry is not generated by AI. This build is a research/teaching visualization interface, not a diagnostic or surgical-planning system.

The browser-optimized atlas is not a claim of exhaustive coverage of every structure in the raw Z-Anatomy source. Full exhaustive coverage would require self-hosting a complete source export and structure-by-structure anatomical QA.
